Syft Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Syft is made by Sylent Software LLC

Syft is a notes app. You write or text it anything, and it files it in the right folder. This page explains what Syft stores, who helps process it, and how to delete it.

What Syft stores

  • Your phone number, to sign you in with a texted code and to know which texts are yours.
  • Your notes and folders, plus small things Syft learns to sort better (for example, that you moved a note to a certain folder).
  • Texts you send to Syft. A text is held only until it is filed into your notes, then deleted. A short log of recent texts and Syft's replies is kept in your account so you can see what it did.
  • Photos you choose to add to a note, only to read what's in them.
  • Gmail connection, if you turn it on: a key from Google that lets Syft create email drafts and send an email only when you reply SEND, and look up a contact's email address when you name them.

Syft does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who helps run Syft

  • Supabase stores your account and notes.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads a note or text to decide where it goes, answer questions about your notes, and write email drafts you ask for. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Sendblue and Twilio deliver texts between your phone and Syft.
  • Google, only if you connect Gmail.
  • Apple turns speech into text when you use the mic button.

Gmail and Google data

Syft's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Syft only uses Gmail and contacts access to draft, revise and send the emails you ask for and to find the address of the person you name. It does not read your inbox, does not share Google data with anyone except to provide this feature, and does not use it for ads or to train AI models. You can disconnect Gmail anytime in Syft's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting your data

In Syft, open Settings (the gear) and tap Delete account. This erases your notes, folders, text history, Gmail connection and account right away. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Syft is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
