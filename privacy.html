Syft Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026 (full Google access, heads-ups and routines added) · Syft is made by Sylent Software LLC

Syft is an assistant you text so nothing slips. Text it anything, and it keeps your notes, to-dos and reminders in order and helps with the accounts you connect. This page explains what Syft stores, who helps process it, and how to delete it.

What Syft stores

  • Your phone number, to sign you in with a texted code and to know which texts are yours.
  • Your notes and folders, plus small things Syft learns to sort better (for example, that you moved a note to a certain folder).
  • Texts you send to Syft. A text is held only until it is filed into your notes, then deleted. A short log of recent texts and Syft's replies is kept in your account so you can see what it did.
  • Photos you choose to add to a note, only to read what's in them.
  • Your phone contacts, if you turn it on: names, phone numbers and emails, so "text Mom" knows who Mom is. You can turn this off in Settings and Syft deletes its copy.
  • Business tools you connect (for example your own dashboard): Syft reads a short summary of your tasks, invoices, meetings and notes there, and adds or updates only what you ask it to.
  • Other apps you connect, if you choose to (for example Slack, Notion or HubSpot): you sign in to that app once, and Syft uses that connection only to do what you text it to, like posting a message or adding a page. Syft asks before sending or posting anything others will see, unless you asked for exactly that.
  • Google connection, if you turn it on: a key from Google that lets Syft work in your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive the way an assistant would: find emails and files you ask about, notice emails still waiting on your reply, draft and send emails (sent only when you reply SEND), read and add calendar events (invites sent only when you reply BOOK), create Google Docs, and look up a contact's email address.

Syft does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who helps run Syft

  • Supabase stores your account and notes.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads a note or text to decide where it goes, answer questions about your notes, and write email drafts you ask for. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Sendblue and Twilio deliver texts between your phone and Syft.
  • Google, only if you connect your Google account.
  • Composio holds the sign-in for other apps you connect (like Slack or Notion) and carries out the actions you ask for in them.
  • Apple turns speech into text when you use the mic button, and delivers your reminders as notifications on your iPhone.

Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive)

Syft's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Syft uses this access only to do what you ask and to give you heads-ups you can turn off: search your email and files when you ask, spot emails from real people still waiting on your reply, see what's coming up on your calendar (like a flight to check in for), draft and send emails you approve, add events you ask for, and create Docs. Syft reads your Google data when it needs to and does not keep copies of your emails or files, apart from short notes of what it already told you about so it doesn't repeat itself. It does not share Google data with anyone except to provide these features, and does not use it for ads or to train AI models. You can disconnect Google anytime in Syft's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting your data

In Syft, open Settings (the gear) and tap Delete account. This erases your notes, folders, text history, Google connection and account right away. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Syft is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
